      Settings

      Cover letters

      A match at or above this score gets a letter written for it automatically. Below it, you can still write one yourself from the Overview or a Pipeline job (always a quick pass).

      A fact-checked letter is drafted, checked against your profile and the ad, and revised up to twice: a few minutes each. The rest get one quick pass with no checks.

      Extras

      Made beside each fact-checked letter (never the quick ones), one model call each and only when there is something to work on: résumé tailoring notes (1–2¢), answers to screening questions in the ad (about 2¢), ways to close gaps you said no to (under 1¢).

      On Seek's "Answer employer questions" step, for jobs with a fact-checked letter: what the job wants beside what your profile has, and a draft answer when you ask for one (1–2¢ each). Off: every question is yours to answer, and no model is called.
